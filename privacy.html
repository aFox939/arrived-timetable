隐私政策

生效及最近更新日期：2026 年 11 月 25 日

本政策适用于 Arrived课表（以下简称“Arrived”或“本 App”）当前的 iPhone App、Apple Watch 伴侣 App、小组件及课程实时活动。开发者为 Arrived课表开发者（aFox939），隐私联系邮箱：fox1723439610@yeah.net。

课程内容主要在你的设备上处理和保存，无需注册 Arrived 账号。
本 App 不会自动将课表、导入文件或课程备注上传到开发者服务器。iPhone 通过 Apple 的 WatchConnectivity 向已配对并安装 Arrived 的 Apple Watch 同步课表快照，不经开发者服务器中转。使用天气、地图、App Store 购买、系统备份或主动发送反馈时，Apple、相关服务提供方或开发者会按下文所述处理相应信息；这不意味着所有功能都不联网，或所有数据始终不会离开设备。

Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: November 25, 2026

This policy applies to the current iPhone app, Apple Watch companion app, widgets, and class Live Activities of Arrived (referred to as “Arrived” or “the App”). The developer is the Arrived developer (aFox939). Privacy contact: fox1723439610@yeah.net.

Class information is primarily processed and stored on your device. You do not need an Arrived account.
The App does not automatically upload timetables, imported files, or class notes to the developer’s servers. The iPhone app syncs timetable snapshots to a paired Apple Watch with Arrived installed through Apple’s WatchConnectivity, without routing them through the developer’s servers. When you use weather, maps, App Store purchases, or system backups, or choose to send feedback, Apple, the relevant service providers, or the developer process the relevant information as described below. This does not mean that every feature works offline or that all data always stays on your device.

1. 我们处理哪些信息及用途

为提供课表、编辑和进度显示，本 App 在设备上处理你输入或导入的课程名称、教师、教室、日期、教学周、学校时区、节次、实际起止时间、作息模板及备注。你还可以选择保存上课地点的名称、地址和坐标。课程与安排标识用于关联编辑、备注及系统提醒，不用于广告追踪。

本 App 也保存必要的使用偏好、提醒及闹钟规则、天气地点和最近天气结果，以及 Apple 验证后的 Pro 权益状态。以上信息用于对应功能，不用于建立广告画像。天气和地图、购买、反馈所涉及的处理分别见下文。

1. What Information We Process and Why

To provide timetable, editing, and progress features, the App processes on your device the course names, instructors, classrooms, dates, teaching weeks, school time zone, class periods, actual start and end times, daily schedule templates, and notes that you enter or import. You can also choose to save the names, addresses, and coordinates of class locations. Course and schedule identifiers associate edits, notes, and system reminders with the relevant entries. They are not used for advertising tracking.

The App also stores necessary preferences, reminder and alarm rules, weather locations and recent weather results, and Pro entitlement status verified by Apple. This information is used for the corresponding features and is not used to build advertising profiles. Processing for weather and maps, purchases, and feedback is described below.

2. 课程存储、导入与本地识别

课表、作息、课程备注和上课地点保存在 App 的本地存储中。为了在保存、导入或旧数据迁移失败时保护课表，设备上可能保留恢复文件、迁移前备份和最近有效快照。删除当前课程或备注不表示所有历史恢复副本立即被物理清除。

当前可用的文件导入为 PDF（包括扫描版 PDF）和 iCalendar（.ics），也可手动录入。App 读取你通过系统文件选择器选定的文件，在设备端使用本地解析和 Apple 的 PDFKit、Vision 等能力提取课程信息，不为解析自动上传文件到开发者或外部识别服务器。

若你选择的文件位于 iCloud Drive 或其他文件提供方，系统可能需要从该提供方下载文件；文件的云端保存、下载与账户管理受该提供方的政策和你的设置控制。导入课表不会自动删除文件提供方保留的原文件。

2. Timetable Storage, Imports, and On-Device Recognition

Timetables, daily schedules, class notes, and class locations are stored locally by the App. To protect your timetable if saving, importing, or migrating older data fails, the device may retain recovery files, pre-migration backups, and the most recent valid snapshots. Deleting a current class or note does not mean that all older recovery copies are physically erased immediately.

The App currently supports importing PDF files, including scanned PDFs, and iCalendar (.ics) files. You can also enter classes manually. The App reads files you select through the system file picker and extracts class information on your device using local parsing and Apple capabilities including PDFKit and Vision. The App does not automatically upload files to the developer or to external recognition servers for parsing.

If the file you select is in iCloud Drive or with another file provider, the system may need to download it from that provider. Cloud storage, downloads, and account management for the file are governed by the provider’s policies and your settings. Importing a timetable does not automatically delete the original file retained by the provider.

3. 可选的 AI 转换指令与剪贴板

“使用 AI 生成 ICS”提供通用提示词的查看与复制功能。点击复制后，App 将该指令写入系统剪贴板，不会自动附上你的课表，也不会自动向 AI 服务发送课程或文件。当前版本未接入 AI 云端识别服务。

如果你自行选择外部 AI 服务并向其提供材料，发送内容和该服务的数据保存、使用及删除方式由你和该服务决定，适用其隐私政策。请先移除不必要的学号、姓名或其他个人信息。剪贴板的访问及可能的系统同步由系统和你的设置管理。

3. Optional AI Conversion Instructions and the Clipboard

“Use AI to Generate ICS” lets you view and copy a generic prompt. When you select Copy, the App writes the prompt to the system clipboard. It does not automatically include your timetable or send classes or files to an AI service. The current version does not integrate a cloud-based AI recognition service.

If you choose an external AI service and provide materials to it, you and that service determine what is sent and how the service retains, uses, and deletes the data. The service’s privacy policy applies. Remove unnecessary student IDs, names, and other personal information first. Clipboard access and any system-level syncing are governed by the system and your settings.

4. 小组件、实时活动与 Apple Watch

为让小组件显示课程、进度和天气，App 通过 Apple App Group 共享容器，在本机与自身扩展共享必要的课程名称、教室、起止时间、进度、天气摘要及 Pro 权益快照。课程备注不写入当前的小组件或课程实时活动快照。这不是向其他开发者的 App 共享课表。

启用实时活动后，App 将必要的课程名称、教室、时间及进度状态交给 ActivityKit，由系统在锁屏、灵动岛和支持的系统界面展示。当前实现不使用开发者服务器远程推送实时活动。系统可能根据设备支持和你的设置，在配对的 Apple Watch 上显示 iPhone 实时活动；这涉及 Apple 管理的配对设备间传递。

在已配对并安装 Arrived 的 Apple Watch 上，iPhone App 会通过 Apple 的 WatchConnectivity 发送课表快照，例如在 iPhone App 进入前台、课表变更或收到手表刷新请求时。快照包含课程名称、教师、教室、课程安排标识、实际起止时间及用于课前显示的时间，也包含课表时区、是否已设置课表、覆盖日期范围、快照生成时间和格式版本。快照覆盖当前周和当前月，并延伸至未来第七天所在的周和月，以便手表 App 和小组件显示课程及计算日、周、月进度。快照不包含课程备注或原始导入文件。配对设备间的传输由 Apple 的 WatchConnectivity 处理，不经开发者服务器中转。

Apple Watch 在本机保存最近成功收到的快照及其接收时间。手表侧 App Group 可用时，Arrived 的 Watch 小组件从该共享容器读取缓存。iPhone 不可达或同步失败时，手表可能继续显示缓存，直到新快照替换。小组件在快照过期后会提示重新同步，过期提示不等于缓存文件已删除。

锁屏、小组件和手表上的课程内容可能被旁人看到。你可通过 App 或系统设置管理实时活动与锁屏显示，也可移除小组件。

4. Widgets, Live Activities, and Apple Watch

To let widgets display classes, progress, and weather, the App uses an Apple App Group shared container to share necessary course names, classrooms, start and end times, progress, weather summaries, and Pro entitlement snapshots with its own extensions on the same device. Class notes are not included in current widget or class Live Activity snapshots. This does not share your timetable with apps from other developers.

When Live Activities are enabled, the App provides the necessary course names, classrooms, times, and progress status to ActivityKit so the system can display them on the Lock Screen, in the Dynamic Island, and on supported system surfaces. The current implementation does not use the developer’s servers to push Live Activities remotely. Depending on device support and your settings, the system may display iPhone Live Activities on a paired Apple Watch. This involves transfers between paired devices managed by Apple.

For a paired Apple Watch with Arrived installed, the iPhone app sends timetable snapshots through Apple’s WatchConnectivity, for example when the iPhone app comes to the foreground, when the timetable changes, or when the watch requests a refresh. The snapshots contain course names, instructors, classrooms, class schedule identifiers, actual start and end times, and times used for display before class. They also include the timetable’s time zone, whether a timetable is configured, the covered date range, when the snapshot was generated, and its format version. The snapshots cover the current week and month and extend through the week and month that contain the date seven days ahead, so the watch app and widgets can show classes and calculate daily, weekly, and monthly progress. The snapshots do not contain class notes or original imported files. Apple’s WatchConnectivity handles transfers between the paired devices without routing them through the developer’s servers.

Apple Watch stores the most recently received snapshot and its receipt time locally. When the App Group on the watch is available, Arrived’s Watch widgets read the cached snapshot from that shared container. If the iPhone is unreachable or syncing fails, the watch may keep displaying cached data until a new snapshot replaces it. Once the snapshot expires, widgets will prompt you to sync again. An expiry notice does not mean that the cached file has been deleted.

People nearby may see class information on your Lock Screen, widgets, or watch. You can manage Live Activities and Lock Screen display in the App or system settings, and you can remove widgets.

5. 课前通知与系统级上课闹钟

课前提醒使用 Apple UserNotifications 的本地调度能力。系统收到用于提醒的课程名称、教室、时间等内容；开启相应天气功能时，提醒也可能包含天气摘要。不通过开发者的推送服务器发送课程提醒。

在 iOS 26 或更新版本、具备相应使用资格并取得系统闹钟授权后，系统级上课闹钟使用 Apple AlarmKit。只有你确认课程、适用范围和提前时间后，App 才提交闹钟，不会默认给整张课表设置闹钟。App 在设备上保存规则及系统闹钟标识，供编辑、取消及核对状态；响铃由系统处理。

你可以在 App 中关闭提醒、关闭或删除已设闹钟，并通过系统设置管理通知、实时活动、闹钟授权及锁屏显示。权限撤销或后台限制可能影响展示和状态同步；关闭功能本身不等于删除已经保存的课表及设置。

5. Before-Class Notifications and System Class Alarms

Before-class reminders use local scheduling through Apple’s UserNotifications. The system receives the course name, classroom, time, and other details needed for each reminder. Reminders may also contain a weather summary when the corresponding weather feature is enabled. Class reminders are not sent through the developer’s push notification servers.

On iOS 26 or later, system class alarms use Apple AlarmKit once you meet the applicable eligibility requirements and grant system alarm permission. The App schedules an alarm only after you confirm the class, which occurrences it applies to, and how far in advance it should ring. It does not set alarms for the entire timetable by default. The App stores rules and system alarm identifiers on your device for editing, cancelling, and checking alarm status. The system handles ringing.

You can turn off reminders and disable or delete scheduled alarms in the App. You can also manage notifications, Live Activities, alarm permission, and Lock Screen display in system settings. Revoking permission or restricting background activity may affect display and status syncing. Turning off a feature does not itself delete saved timetables or settings.

6. 天气、位置权限与地图

天气功能调用 Apple WeatherKit。选择“当前位置”时，App 按需请求系统的使用期间位置权限并获取位置；实际位置精度由系统授权及设备情况决定。位置坐标会交给 Apple 天气服务以获取对应地点的预报。本 App 不为天气持续进行后台轨迹追踪。

你也可手动选择天气地点；这不需要当前位置授权，但地点坐标仍用于天气查询。搜索天气地点和上课地点时，搜索词及相关地点信息由 Apple MapKit 地图服务处理。展示上课地点地图，或点击在 Apple 地图中打开时，相应坐标、地点名称等会用于 Apple 地图服务。

所选天气地点及最近结果在本机缓存，必要的天气摘要供本 App 的小组件和提醒使用。为展示 Apple 天气署名及相关资源，部分页面也可能向 Apple 加载资源。开发者不通过自己的服务器接收这些位置、搜索词或天气查询。Apple 的相关处理适用 Apple 隐私政策、地图与隐私说明及天气服务中的 法律归属说明。

你可在系统设置中撤销 Arrived 的位置权限，或选择手动地点。拒绝定位不影响本地课表查看、编辑与文件解析；撤销权限不会自动清除已选地点或历史本地缓存，也不等于停止手动地点的天气查询。

6. Weather, Location Permission, and Maps

The weather feature uses Apple WeatherKit. When you select “Current Location,” the App requests permission from the system as needed to access your location while you use the App, and obtains your location. Actual location accuracy depends on system permissions and your device. Location coordinates are sent to Apple’s weather service to obtain the forecast for that location. The App does not continuously track your movements in the background for weather.

You can also select a weather location manually. This does not require current location permission, but the selected location’s coordinates are still used for weather requests. When you search for weather or class locations, Apple’s MapKit service processes your search terms and relevant location information. When you view a map of a class location or choose to open it in Apple Maps, the relevant coordinates, place name, and similar information are used by Apple’s map services.

The selected weather location and recent results are cached on your device. The App’s widgets and reminders use weather summaries as needed. Some pages may also load resources from Apple to display Apple Weather attribution and related resources. The developer does not receive these locations, search terms, or weather requests through the developer’s own servers. Apple’s processing is governed by the Apple Privacy Policy, Maps & Privacy, and the weather service’s legal attribution information.

You can revoke Arrived’s location permission in system settings or select a location manually. Denying location permission does not affect viewing or editing local timetables or parsing files. Revoking permission does not automatically clear selected locations or older local caches, and it does not stop weather requests for locations chosen manually.

Arrived Pro 的月度、年度订阅和永久买断通过 Apple App Store 与 StoreKit 2 提供。Apple 处理购买、付款、订阅管理、恢复购买、退款和交易验证。App 读取 Apple 提供并验证的必要商品、交易标识、订阅有效期及撤销状态等信息，用于判断和更新权益，并在设备及自身扩展中缓存必要的权益状态。

App 不要求你向开发者提供 Apple 账户密码或银行卡信息，也没有独立的开发者支付系统。同一 Apple 账户的权益恢复由 Apple 服务处理，不代表课表会随之同步。删除 App 不会自动取消订阅或删除 Apple 保存的交易记录；订阅与购买记录需通过 Apple 提供的渠道管理。详见 App Store 与隐私。

7. Subscriptions, Lifetime Purchases, and Restoring Purchases

Arrived Pro’s monthly and annual subscriptions and one-time lifetime purchase are offered through the Apple App Store and StoreKit 2. Apple handles purchases, payments, subscription management, purchase restoration, refunds, and transaction verification. The App reads necessary information supplied and verified by Apple, such as product and transaction identifiers, subscription expiration dates, and revocation status, to determine and update entitlements. It caches the necessary entitlement status on your device and in its own extensions.

The App does not ask you to provide your Apple Account password or payment card information to the developer, and the developer does not operate a separate payment system. Apple services restore entitlements for the same Apple Account. This does not sync your timetable. Deleting the App does not automatically cancel a subscription or delete transaction records held by Apple. Manage subscriptions and purchase records through Apple’s services. See App Store & Privacy.

The developer does not sell class or location data or use it for advertising tracking across apps and websites. To provide the features you choose, necessary data is provided to Apple system services or file providers as described above, or you may choose to send it to external services or the developer. The App does not create a copy of your timetable on the developer’s servers.

Local App data, preferences, caches, and recovery copies are retained on your device until they are updated in the normal course, deleted through an applicable feature, or cleared by the system. Depending on your device settings, they may be included in system backups or device migrations. Backup retention, restoration, and deletion are controlled by the relevant device and Apple service settings. The developer cannot access your system backups. Timetable snapshots sync from iPhone to a paired Apple Watch with Arrived installed through Apple’s WatchConnectivity and are cached on the watch, without being routed through the developer’s servers. The App does not enable any developer-operated cross-device cloud timetable syncing.

The App uses mechanisms such as system sandboxing, shared containers, and file read and write protections to handle local data. Recovery mechanisms for saving and migration reduce the risk of loss, but cannot guarantee absolute security for any device, storage, or network environment. We recommend protecting your device and reviewing materials before sending feedback or sharing them with another service.
