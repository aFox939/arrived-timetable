Make it yours.

从一份课表开始。

  1. 01　导入或新建

    支持 PDF、扫描版 PDF 与 iCalendar（.ics）文件，也可以手动添加课程。

  2. 02　核对与修改

    导入后在课表中核对信息，打开课程详情即可修改。识别结果可能有误，请确认日期和节次。

  3. 03　按自己的节奏

    设置课前提醒与实时活动；在支持的配对 Apple Watch 上查看系统实时活动。课程使用课表设置的学校时区。

Make it yours.

Start with a timetable.

  1. 01　Import or create

    Import a PDF, including a scanned PDF, or an iCalendar (.ics) file. You can also add classes manually.

  2. 02　Review and edit

    After importing, review your timetable and open the class details to make edits. Recognition can make mistakes, so check the dates and class periods.

  3. 03　Set your own pace

    Set before-class reminders and Live Activities. View system Live Activities on a supported paired Apple Watch. Classes use the school time zone set in your timetable.

需要注册账号吗？

不需要。课表与备注保存在设备本地，核心功能无需登录。

能把任意课表完全准确地导入吗？

不同文件的表格结构和清晰度会影响识别。请在导入后核对课程、星期、教学周、节次和教室，必要时直接修改。

支持什么语言与设备？

App 支持简体中文与英文，并跟随系统语言，适用于 iOS 17 及以上的 iPhone。系统级上课闹钟需要 iOS 26 或更新版本；配对 Apple Watch 的实时活动显示取决于系统支持。Arrived 提供 Apple Watch 伴侣 App，课表快照会从 iPhone 同步到已配对的 Apple Watch。

Do I need to create an account?

No. Timetables and notes are kept locally on your device, and the core features work without signing in.

Can every timetable be imported perfectly?

Table layouts and file clarity affect recognition. After importing, check classes, days of the week, teaching weeks, class periods, and classrooms, and edit them as needed.

Which languages and devices are supported?

The app supports Simplified Chinese and English, follows your system language, and works on iPhone with iOS 17 or later. System class alarms require iOS 26 or later. Live Activities on a paired Apple Watch depend on system support. Arrived includes an Apple Watch companion app. Timetable snapshots sync from iPhone to a paired Apple Watch.

关于数据与隐私

阅读完整隐私政策 ↗

无需注册 Arrived 账号。课表、作息与备注主要在本机保存，PDF / ICS 解析不自动上传文件。小组件通过本机共享容器读取必要快照；系统可在配对 Watch 上展示 iPhone 实时活动。iPhone 通过 Apple 的 WatchConnectivity 向已配对并安装 Arrived 的 Apple Watch 同步课表快照，不经开发者服务器中转。

天气查询、地点搜索及地图展示使用 Apple WeatherKit / MapKit；相关地点坐标、搜索词会由 Apple 服务处理。Pro 购买、订阅和恢复通过 Apple App Store。通知与系统级上课闹钟使用 Apple 本地系统能力。复制 AI 指令不自动发送课表；外部 AI 由你自行选择。详情请阅读完整政策。

App 未接入广告、追踪或第三方分析 SDK。系统诊断、TestFlight、主动支持邮件及系统备份仍可能涉及相应处理。本网站未添加统计或追踪脚本；GitHub Pages 的托管处理见 GitHub 隐私声明。隐私问题请联系支持邮箱。

Data and privacy

Read the full Privacy Policy ↗

You do not need an Arrived account. Timetables, daily schedules, and notes are mainly stored on your device. Parsing PDF or ICS files does not automatically upload them. Widgets read the necessary snapshots from a local shared container. The system can show iPhone Live Activities on a paired Apple Watch. The iPhone app syncs timetable snapshots to a paired Apple Watch with Arrived installed through Apple’s WatchConnectivity, without routing them through the developer’s servers.

Weather, location searches, and maps use Apple WeatherKit and MapKit. Apple services process relevant location coordinates and search terms. Pro purchases, subscriptions, and restorations go through the Apple App Store. Notifications and system class alarms use Apple’s on-device system features. Copying AI instructions does not automatically send your timetable. You choose whether to use an external AI service. See the full policy for details.

The app does not include ads, tracking, or third-party analytics SDKs. System diagnostics, TestFlight, support emails you send, and system backups may still involve data processing. This site has no added analytics or tracking scripts. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement for information about GitHub Pages hosting. Email support with privacy questions.
